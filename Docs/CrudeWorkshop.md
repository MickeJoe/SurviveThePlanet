# Crude Workshop

The starter workshop reuses the existing `CrudeWorkshop` catalog identity and Industry toolbar entry. Its blueprint is known from colony start; construction costs 10 iron ore, occupies 4x4 cells, and uses the normal controller placement, construction-drone queue and power grid. Other starting blueprints are unchanged.

Editable model: `ContentSource/CrudeWorkshop/CrudeWorkshop.blend`. The off-white/orange prefab shell has an open workshop bay, bench, press, stock bins, ventilation and service panels. The body, fan and press ram are separate exported meshes. Imported content and the functional Blueprint are in `/Game/Units/Buildings/CrudeWorkshop`. The meshes reuse the existing Geothermal textured paint/metal materials (base color, roughness, normal and AO) and Energy Extender cyan indicator material; no duplicate material graphs were introduced. The previous display-only catalog Blueprint remains available for compatibility; the existing data asset points new construction at the functional Blueprint.

`ACrudeWorkshop` uses the existing atomic `UResourceProductionComponent` and the shared building panel. It offers Concrete (4 stone, 120 seconds), Steel (4 iron ore, 180 seconds), and Copper metal (3 copper ore, 150 seconds), one output per cycle at 100% total drone efficiency. Circuit Boards are excluded. Four drone slots are unlocked; assigned Construction efficiency scales production. New workshops begin paused and require Start, staffing, power and materials. Work automatically repeats, waits through starvation, and resumes when supplied. Pause preserves progress; changing recipe clears unfinished work without spending materials. All recipes use the existing 6 electricity/minute setting. See [ProductionPanel.md](ProductionPanel.md) for the shared UI and behavior.

The fan and press move only during actual production. `UIndustrialEffectsComponent` supplies a small wind-driven steam plume and pulsing process light, also gated by actual production. Missing materials, unfinished construction, lack of grid coverage or empty power stop the work effects. Existing low night safety lights are independent. The HUD includes workshop output and input rates. Game pause and speed use ordinary world delta time.

The shared UMG popup now provides recipe selection, inline missing-input warnings, real cycle progress, four drone cards, and Start/Pause controls. There is no Repeat control or separate waiting banner.

Reproduction scripts: `Scripts/create_crude_workshop_blender.py` runs through native Blender MCP; `Scripts/import_crude_workshop_ue.py` imports and registers the assets in the canonical UE 5.8 editor. `Scripts/create_production_panel_ue.py` authors the shared UMG panel/cards. `Scripts/test_crude_workshop_ue.py` delegates to `Scripts/test_production_panel_ue.py` for the current recipe, drone, pause/resume, power, starvation and UI regression checks. The native `SurviveThePlanet.UI.BlueprintToolbar` test covers the actual controller placement, terrain reservations, construction payment and drone job queue.
